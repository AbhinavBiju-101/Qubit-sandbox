BONUS · TUNE FOR 75% / 25%

Skewing the coin

A Hadamard always gives 50/50 because it rotates exactly to the equator of the Bloch sphere. To land anywhere else, rotate by a smaller angle instead — that's what RY(θ) does. For P(0) ≈ 75%, solve cos²(θ/2) = 0.75, which gives θ ≈ 60°.
