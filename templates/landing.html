WHY THIS EXISTS

Most explanations stop at the analogy

"A qubit is like a coin that's both heads and tails" is where most explainers end. Qubit Sandbox is the next step down — the actual amplitudes, the actual matrices, the actual measurement statistics — so the analogy stops being necessary. Built for a quantum computing hackathon problem set, and kept running afterward as an open, self-contained learning tool.
